Photo repair

Rebuilds display sizes that failed during busy uploads

When lots of photos arrive at once, a few display sizes fail to build. Guests still see every photo, but those few load slowly the first time someone opens them. Switching the site to Live turns this on for you, and it repairs them every 15 minutes. Turn it off yourself once uploads stop — there is nothing left to repair after that.
